The research

A Flashpacker in my family has taken three trips with you. I asked her to book a fourth on her phone and talk me through every trip. Here it is, raw. Personal details are cut.

What I found

  • The people sell the trip. She checks who's booked before she picks a date.
  • Checkout hides them. Who's going sits behind a sign-up, and the roommate match comes last.
  • Mobile asks too much. She moved to her laptop to book.

The solution

Your brief: take checkout, make it simpler, make it feel like Flash Pack, and say what you cut.

Seven cuts. Nothing new.

The people move to the front. Everything else gets out of their way.

Today · mobile checkout

  1. Four urgency signals per dateCut
  2. Sign-up to see who's goingCut
  3. Blank screen after Book NowCut
  4. Promo code firstCut
  5. Extras, payment plan, billing
  6. Terms and conditions wallCut
  7. Trip summary and roommate matchBuried
  8. Chat prompt on every screenCut
